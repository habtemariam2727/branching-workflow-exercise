# Guacamole recipe


## Ingredients

- 2 avocados
- 1 chili
- 1 lime
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 onion
- 1 Tbsp cilantro


## Instructions

- Prep the Avocados: Slice the avocados in half, remove the pits, and scoop the flesh into a medium bowl. Dice them into bite-sized cubes, or mash them with a fork depending on your preferred texture (chunky vs. smooth).

- Finely Dice the Aromatics: Finely dice the white or red onion and the chili pepper (remember to remove the seeds if you prefer less heat). Toss these into the bowl with the avocado.

- Citrus & Seasoning: Cut the lime in half and squeeze the fresh juice directly over the mixture. The acid not only adds a bright flavor but also keeps the avocado from turning brown. Sprinkle a generous pinch of salt over everything.

- Combine Gently: Using a spoon or spatula, fold the ingredients together until well combined. Try not to overmix if you want to keep some of that nice, chunky texture.

- Taste and Adjust: Take a quick taste with a chip or spoon. Need more zing? Add another squeeze of lime. More punch? Toss in another pinch of salt.

- Serve & Enjoy: Transfer your fresh guacamole to a serving bowl and enjoy immediately with your favorite tortilla chips, tacos, or as a spread!
